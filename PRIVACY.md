# GTA 6 Spoiler Shield Privacy Policy

The extension processes post text, image alternative text, and video titles on X and YouTube in your browser to apply the spoiler filter. It does not save the page content it checks or transmit that content off your device.

Your keyword list and filter preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension does not share or sell this data or use it for advertising. You can change your list and preferences from the extension popup. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data.

The extension's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
